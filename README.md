Data Science student at IIT Madras, based in Jaipur. I build small apps to fix things that annoy me, then I actually ship them. My phone kept showing 5G on a 4G tower, so I built something that reads the real signal. I wanted notes that gave nothing away if someone grabbed my phone, so I built one that hides them as code.

## 📲 Install and try

**[TrueG](https://github.com/RudraO2/TrueG)** tells you whether your phone is really on 5G or just showing the icon. [Download the APK](https://github.com/RudraO2/TrueG/releases).

**[6 Months Left](https://github.com/RudraO2/six-months-left)** is a countdown app with teeth: it blocks your time-sink apps until a deadline you set, and will not let you casually uninstall it before then. [Download the APK](https://github.com/RudraO2/six-months-left/releases).

## 🧩 Also built

**[Daily Notes](https://github.com/RudraO2/daily-notes)** looks like an ordinary notes app and stores every entry disguised as code, so a glance at your screen gives nothing away.

**[NSE Screener](https://github.com/RudraO2/nse-screener)** reads where the Indian market sits in its cycle, then ranks momentum stocks inside the sectors money is flowing into.

## 🏗️ Bigger builds

**[tokenbrawl](https://github.com/RudraO2/tokenbrawl)** is a benchmark where two language models fight inside a game. The engine waits for each model to answer, so answering fast buys nothing; what runs out is a token budget that drains while a model thinks. Eight machine-checked rules keep every match fair and repeatable.

**[Hydra-Db-Hack](https://github.com/RudraO2/Hydra-Db-Hack)** is an open-world office game where the characters actually remember things. They gossip, recall what you told them, and build their own theory about a missing drive.

**[Nirman CRM](https://github.com/RudraO2/Nirman-crm)** is a simple CRM I built and shipped to a real estate business in Jaipur, designed so a 45-year-old sales rep gets it right on the first try with no training.
